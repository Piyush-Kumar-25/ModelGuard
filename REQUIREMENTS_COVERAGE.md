## Interpretive limits

“Covered” means the requested behavior is implemented and checked at the level stated here. It does not mean statistical validity is universal, that Auto always chooses an ensemble, that every small temporal/group/class dataset supports calibration, that every candidate finishes within its budget, or that an unlabelled monitoring batch proves predictive stability. No source test fixture is presented as production evidence.
